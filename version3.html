Did you know?
Magnesium oxide (MgO) is produced by calcining magnesite (MgCO₃) at high temperatures. Both MgCO₃ (raw magnesite) and MgO are available from our Ethiopian mines.
  • MgCO₃ (Magnesite): The raw mineral, easier and less costly to ship in bulk.
  • MgO (Magnesium Oxide): A high-value processed material (from MgCO₃) used in refractories, agriculture, and industry. However, exporting MgO involves higher energy and processing costs as well as increased shipping costs due to its higher density and value-add. For this reason, orders for MgO are best suited to buyers who require premium quality and can justify a higher value return.
For international buyers: If your application requires raw magnesite (MgCO₃), you benefit from lower shipping costs. If you need processed MgO, you receive a ready-to-use, high-grade product, but the total delivered cost will be higher—so please ensure your project or process requires the full value of premium MgO.

Dolomite

Uses:

Uses:

  • Environmental (neutralizing acidic waste)

Magnesite (MgCO₃)

Uses:
  • Raw material for MgO production
  • Refractories
  • Industrial filler, ceramics, glass
